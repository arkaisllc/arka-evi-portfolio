# Example EVI review summary

**Report reviewed:** H1 P&L variance report  
**Audience:** Executive and finance leadership  
**Primary decision:** Determine which performance drivers require corrective action  
**Data classification:** Fictional demonstration data

## Assessment

The current table is useful for reconciliation, but it asks leadership to scan sixty-plus figures before finding the operating-income gap. Color indicates negative accounting values instead of adverse business performance, and the largest drivers are not ranked.

## Recommended change

Use a concise H1 P&L summary for the executive view. Preserve the familiar financial hierarchy, compare actual with budget, show operating margin, and add a ranked view of the variances contributing to the gap.

## Proposed headline

**Operating income is $0.57M below budget**

## Priority actions

1. Confirm the sources of the $0.33M revenue shortfall.
2. Separate volume, price, and mix effects where the data supports them.
3. Review payroll and marketing commitments against the revised revenue outlook.
4. Keep monthly account detail available as a supporting schedule.

This is an illustrative EVI deliverable. Actual finance reporting should follow the organization's accounting definitions and materiality thresholds.
