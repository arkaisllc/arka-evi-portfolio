# Example EVI review summary

**Report reviewed:** Annual sales by category  
**Audience:** Business leadership  
**Primary decision:** Identify leading categories and understand the distance between them  
**Data classification:** Fictional demonstration data

## Overall assessment

The report is numerically accurate, but the fifteen-slice pie chart makes category comparison slower than necessary. The reader must match colors to a legend and compare similar angles. The visual also gives every category a different color even though color does not encode a business condition.

## Recommended change

Replace the pie chart with descending horizontal bars. Label each category directly, show values in millions to one decimal place, and use the title to communicate the main finding. Preserve exact values in an accompanying table or export.

## Priority actions

1. Change the visual from a pie chart to horizontal bars.
2. Sort categories in descending order.
3. Remove the separate legend and label categories directly.
4. Replace the fifteen-color palette with one brand color.
5. Round visible labels while retaining exact source values.
6. Rewrite the title as a supported finding.

## Proposed title

**Electronics and Furniture lead sales; the top five categories contribute 64%**

## Expected result

Readers can identify the rank, relative scale, and leading group without reconstructing the comparison from slices and a legend. The report continues to show every category and preserves the complete underlying data.

## Follow-up questions

- Should the report emphasize share of revenue, absolute sales, or both?
- Does leadership need to compare this ranking with a target or prior period?
- Are any smaller categories strategically important enough to highlight?
- Should users be able to drill into products, regions, or time periods?

This is an illustrative sample of an EVI review deliverable. Actual reviews are adapted to the report's audience, decision, platform, and implementation constraints.
