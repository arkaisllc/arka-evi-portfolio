# Design rationale: executive revenue dashboard

## Reporting objective

Help leadership understand the H1 revenue and margin gaps, identify where revenue is behind plan, and choose the first follow-up action.

## Diagnostic observations

- Eight cards create a flat hierarchy and make secondary metrics compete with revenue and margin.
- A regional-share pie chart answers where revenue comes from, while the management question concerns performance against plan.
- Monthly columns show growth but omit the plan required to assess performance.
- The margin gauge consumes space without stating the 1.6-point target gap.
- Exact cents suggest precision beyond the executive decision being made.

## Redesign specification

- Lead with the supported finding that revenue is 3.9% below plan.
- Retain revenue, gross margin, and six-month change as the core KPIs.
- Compare monthly actual revenue directly with plan.
- Show regional variance around a zero line so positive and negative performance are distinguishable.
- Use rounded millions in the visual and retain exact figures in supporting data.
- State the next management question in a decision panel.

## Validation

Actual H1 revenue totals $12.40M and plan totals $12.90M. The shortfall is $0.50M, or 3.88%, rounded to 3.9%. Regional actual and plan values reconcile to those totals.
