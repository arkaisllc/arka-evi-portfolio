# Example EVI review summary

**Report reviewed:** Executive revenue dashboard  
**Audience:** Business leadership  
**Primary decision:** Identify the performance gap and select the first area for follow-up  
**Data classification:** Fictional demonstration data

## Assessment

The current dashboard contains useful measures, but its flat card layout and unrelated visual forms do not create a clear path from performance to action. Regional share, monthly growth, and margin appear separately, so leadership must reconstruct the gap and its likely location.

## Recommended change

Organize the page around revenue versus plan. Retain three core signals, compare monthly actuals with plan, rank regional variances around zero, and state the next decision explicitly.

## Proposed headline

**Revenue is 3.9% below plan; West has the largest shortfall**

## Priority actions

1. Validate the West pipeline and major account movement.
2. Review whether margin mix is contributing to the shortfall.
3. Preserve order and customer details as drill-through information.
4. Add forecast confidence once a consistent forecast measure is available.

This is an illustrative EVI deliverable. Actual recommendations depend on the audience, operating process, and available data.
