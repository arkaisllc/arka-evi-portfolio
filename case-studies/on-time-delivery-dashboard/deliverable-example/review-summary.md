# Example EVI review summary

**Report reviewed:** On-time delivery dashboard  
**Audience:** Operations leadership  
**Primary decision:** Focus corrective action on the causes most responsible for declining service  
**Data classification:** Fictional demonstration data

## Assessment

The current dashboard shows several service measures and individual late orders, but it does not make the sustained decline or cause concentration clear. Repeated gauges consume space, arrows omit a comparison period, and the table requires manual classification before it supports action.

## Recommended change

Center the page on OTIF trend versus target. Replace repeated gauges with two direct measures, aggregate late-order reasons into ranked causes, and connect the two largest causes to a specific operating review.

## Proposed headline

**On-time delivery fell 4.6 points; two causes drive 61% of exceptions**

## Priority actions

1. Review West carrier capacity by lane and promised-delivery date.
2. Compare warehouse picking windows with carrier cutoff times.
3. Assign owners and due dates to the largest exception categories.
4. Keep order-level detail available for root-cause confirmation.

This is an illustrative EVI deliverable. Actual operational recommendations require validated reason codes and clear ownership definitions.
